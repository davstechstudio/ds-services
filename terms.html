TERMS OF SERVICE

The rules for using SkillConnect GH — for customers booking professionals and professionals offering services.

Effective date: 28 September 2026 · SkillConnect GH ("SkillConnect", "we", "us") operates the marketplace at this website, matching customers with verified independent professionals in Ghana.

1. What SkillConnect is — and is not

SkillConnect is a marketplace and escrow platform. We vet professionals (Ghana Card and identity checks) and hold payments in escrow, but the services are performed by independent professionals, not by SkillConnect. Each professional is solely responsible for the quality, safety and legality of the work they perform.

2. Booking, quotes and escrow

  • Submitting a booking request is free. Professionals quote at their own listed or negotiated rates; accepting a quote books the professional.
  • Payment is made into escrow, held by SkillConnect. The professional must not ask you to pay outside the platform; such payments are not protected by escrow, refunds or disputes.
  • Funds are released to the professional when you approve the completed job, or automatically resolved through the dispute process. A service commission (currently 10%) is deducted from the released amount.
  • Receipts for every escrow payment and release are available on the tracking page.

3. Disputes, refunds and rematches

If a professional does not show up or the service is materially below what was agreed, open a dispute from the tracking page within 48 hours of the scheduled job. Our resolution team responds within 24 hours and may issue a refund, partially refund, or rematch another verified professional at the held amount. Dispute decisions on escrowed funds are final.

4. Professional obligations

  • Keep your identity documents, contact details and rates accurate; misrepresentation voids your verification.
  • Quote honestly — demanding more than the agreed quote after acceptance is grounds for removal.
  • Registration and identity-verification fees, where applicable, cover vetting costs and are non-refundable once review has begun.
  • Wallet balances represent released escrow minus commission and may be withdrawn to the registered Mobile Money number.

5. Prohibited use

  • Circumventing escrow or taking payment off-platform.
  • Fraudulent bookings, chargeback abuse, harassment, or using the platform for anything illegal under the laws of Ghana.
  • Scraping, probing, or interfering with the platform's security or other users' data.

6. Liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, SkillConnect's total liability for any claim relating to a booking is limited to the escrow amount held for that booking. We are not liable for indirect or consequential losses. Nothing in these terms excludes liability that cannot be excluded under Ghanaian law.

7. Suspension and termination

We may suspend or remove any account that breaches these terms, submits fraudulent documents, or creates risk for other users. Escrowed funds for completed work remain payable to the professional; funds for undelivered work are refunded to the customer.
