Pricing FAQs
Why give ranges instead of fixed prices?
Skilled work varies with guest count, hours, travel and season. Ranges set fair expectations while pros quote your exact job — the assistant flags any quote that drifts outside the market range.
Can I negotiate with a professional?
Yes — agree the scope together. Just know the fair range first (that's this page), and never pay outside escrow: payments made outside the platform are not protected.
Do prices include transport and materials?
For events, confirm setup/transport inclusions in the quote. For handy jobs, call-out is usually in the visit rate while parts are quoted separately before purchase.
What about peak seasons like December?
December ("Detty December") demand can push top event vendors to the upper end of their range or booked out early. For December events, book 4–8 weeks ahead.
