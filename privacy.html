PRIVACY POLICY

What we collect, why we collect it, and how your data is protected.

Effective date: 28 September 2026 · This policy explains how SkillConnect GH handles personal data for customers, professionals and applicants, in line with Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

1. What we collect

  • Customers: name, phone number, booking details (service, date, location), chat messages, reviews and dispute statements.
  • Professionals & applicants: name, phone/WhatsApp, city, service category, rates, portfolio, and identity documents (Ghana Card images) submitted for verification.
  • Transaction data: escrow amounts, Mobile Money or card references, commissions, payouts — stored as financial records.
  • Technical data: session cookies (strictly necessary, httpOnly) and device-recent-booking references stored on your own device.

2. What we do NOT collect

We never see or store your Mobile Money PIN or card numbers — payments run through the gateway (MTN MoMo / Paystack) and we hold only references and amounts. Passwords are stored only as salted scrypt hashes; identity-document images are visible only to our verification team.

3. How your data is used

  • To operate the service: matching, quoting, escrow, chat, receipts, dispute resolution and payouts.
  • To verify professionals before their profile goes live.
  • To contact you about your booking, application, or account (WhatsApp/SMS/phone where you provided the number).
  • Financial records (escrow movements, commission) are retained as required for accounting and tax.

4. Who we share it with

  • Payment processors (MTN Mobile Money, Paystack) — strictly the data needed to move and reference money.
  • The counterparty to a booking — professionals see the job location and service details; customers see the professional's profile. Phone numbers stay hidden until a participant chooses to share them in chat.
  • Authorities — where compelled by law or to prevent fraud.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not run advertising trackers.

5. Storage and security

  • Data is stored in an access-controlled database; sessions use httpOnly Secure cookies; all traffic is served over HTTPS.
  • Passwords: salted scrypt hashes. Identity documents: restricted to the verification team.
  • The platform is backed up daily; backups retain the same protections.

6. Retention

Booking and financial records are kept for at least 6 years (accounting requirement). Unsuccessful applications are retained for 12 months unless you ask us to delete them sooner. Identity documents of verified professionals are retained while the profile is live.

7. Your rights

You may request a copy, correction or deletion of your personal data, or withdraw consent for marketing contact, by messaging 024 421 1595 on WhatsApp. We respond within 30 days. You may also complain to the Data Protection Commission of Ghana.

8. Changes

Material changes to this policy will be announced on the site. See also our Terms of Service.
