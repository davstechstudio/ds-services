Frequently Asked Questions
How much does it cost to use SkillConnect as a customer?
Nothing. Browsing, requesting quotes and support are free for customers. You only pay the agreed service price — held in escrow until the job is done.
How do I know the professionals are genuine?
Every pro passes ID verification (Ghana Card, Passport, Driver's License or Voter ID), skill and portfolio checks before their profile goes live, and only customers who completed a paid booking can leave reviews.
What if the professional doesn't show up?
Your escrow payment is protected. Our resolution team will rematch you with another verified pro or refund you — you never lose money to a no-show.
How fast can I get someone in an emergency?
Emergency requests (leaking pipe, dead sound system mid-event, etc.) go to standby pros on priority dispatch — expect contact within 30 minutes during support hours (8AM–9PM daily).
Can I talk to the professional before booking?
Yes. Once quotes arrive you chat directly with each pro on WhatsApp or by phone, ask questions and agree the exact scope before paying anything.
Which areas do you cover?
We are headquartered in Cantonment-Labone, Accra, and match pros nationwide — Greater Accra, Ashanti (Kumasi), Western (Takoradi), Central, Eastern and Volta regions, with more cities added every month.
